# Content and publishing policy

TrendForge is an original commentary production system, not a video-reposting bot.

## Permitted inputs and outputs

- Fetch publicly available YouTube Data API metadata: video title, creator/channel name, public counts, publication time, category, description, and public URL.
- Use the public metadata as a signal for a new editorial angle about creator strategy, audience attention or the broader creator economy.
- Produce an original script, synthetic narration, abstract motion graphics, captions, and metadata.
- Link the source video in the description when the discussion is based on that source's observable metadata.

## Prohibited by design

- Do not download or store source videos, audio, thumbnails, or captions using scraping, third-party downloaders, or undocumented endpoints.
- Do not synthesize a creator's voice, create fake footage of a real person, imply the creator endorsed the Short, or fabricate quotes, earnings or motives.
- Do not make claims about what happens inside a source video unless that fact is independently available and verified.
- Do not copy a source transcript or produce a near-verbatim summary of a copyrighted work.
- Do not stuff unrelated creator names, hashtags or keywords into titles/tags.
- Do not publish the same angle repeatedly, even if the source title differs.

## Originality gate

Each narration should contain a clear, useful perspective beyond reciting metrics. The script generator must stay within the supplied facts, identify its analysis as a hypothesis where appropriate, and use a separate editorial angle. A quality self-score and word-count gate are applied before rendering. These automated checks reduce risk but cannot guarantee accuracy, originality, copyright compliance or monetization acceptance.

## Hashtags, tags and descriptions

- Include #Shorts in the description.
- Prefer a few relevant hashtags, not a long hashtag wall.
- Use tags related to the exact creator/topic only when relevant. No misleading creator impersonation or unrelated celebrity keywords.
- Describe the video as original commentary based on public metadata, and link sources.

## Synthetic media

The rendered visuals are original abstract motion graphics, and the voice is generated from our own script. The pipeline does not generate photorealistic scenes or impersonate real creators. If the product is later changed to include realistic synthetic depictions of real people, places or events, the uploader's synthetic-media disclosure behavior must be updated and tested before automated publication. Always follow the current YouTube disclosure requirements.

## Platform compliance and monetization

YouTube can evaluate a channel's content as a whole. Rights permission alone does not guarantee monetization: repetitive/mass-produced content and minimally transformed reused content can be ineligible. Review the current official policies before changing the format or increasing volume. This repository is not legal advice.
